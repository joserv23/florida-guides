Florida Spring Turkey Quota Permits: How to Apply (2026–27)

If you want to hunt spring turkey on a Florida wildlife management area (WMA), most WMAs require a quota permit — and November is when you apply. The permit itself is free. What costs you is missing the window: Phase I applications for the 2026–27 season close November 30, 2026.

Deadline: Phase I spring turkey quota applications close November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. Missed it? Phase II runs December 4–14, 2026, and Phase III leftovers open December 17 — see the windows below.

What a quota permit is

Florida's quota program caps the number of hunters on many WMAs so an area doesn't get overrun. A quota permit is a limited-entry permit for a specific WMA and hunt. Quota permits are issued at no cost, but unless you are exempt, you must already hold a valid Florida Management Area Permit (or a license that includes one) to apply. (Source: FWC, Limited Entry / Quota Hunts)

The 2026–27 application windows (Spring Turkey Quota)

  • Phase I — random drawing: November 1–30, 2026
  • Phase II — random drawing: December 4–14, 2026
  • Phase III — leftovers, first-come, first-served: from December 17, 2026, until filled or the final hunt date

All application periods open at 10 a.m. Eastern on the first day and close at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on the last day. Youth Spring Turkey quota permits follow the same three phases. Quail quota Phase I runs November 20–30, 2026, with Phase II December 4–14, 2026. (Source: FWC, Limited Entry Hunt Application Periods and Deadlines)

How to apply

  1. Sign in to your Go Outdoors Florida account at GoOutdoorsFlorida.com and choose "Apply for Limited Entry/Quota Permits" — or complete an application worksheet and present it to any license agent or tax collector's office.
  2. List your hunt choices (up to five choices for spring turkey quota, per FWC).
  3. Drawing results are typically posted within three business days after the application period ends. Permits are not available to print or sync until 10 days before the hunt begins.
  4. If you miss the drawing, watch Phase III leftovers from December 17 — they go first-come, first-served until filled.

Do you even need a quota permit?

Not always. Around 40 WMAs don't require a quota permit to hunt spring turkey for the entire season, part of the season, or a portion of the area, and some issue daily quota permits first-come, first-served at the check station. But season dates, bag limits, legal methods, and quota rules are set in each WMA's own regulations brochure — the statewide dates don't apply on WMAs. Check the brochure for the exact area you want to hunt before you apply or go. (Source: FWC, 2026 Spring Turkey Hunt Guide)

What you need before you apply

  • A Florida hunting license (unless exempt)
  • A turkey permit
  • A Management Area Permit (unless exempt) — required to apply for the quota permit
  • Your Go Outdoors Florida account
